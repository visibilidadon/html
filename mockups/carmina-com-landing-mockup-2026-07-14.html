Our heritage

Carmina Shoemaker began as a small family workshop on the island of Mallorca. Five generations later, the same hands-on methods — Goodyear welt construction, hand-lasting, and meticulous leather selection — still shape every pair that leaves our workshop.

We build shoes to be resoled, not replaced: an investment in construction over trend, made by artisans who learned the craft from the generation before them.
